How to use this binder in the exam

  1. Translate the question with Tab G. The keywords that decide the method are paling sedikit, paling banyak, tidak lebih dari, jika diketahui, tanpa pengembalian, rata-rata … per.
  2. Classify it with Tab I (index) or Tab D (flowchart). This tells you the distribution/rule and the page.
  3. Compute with the worked example on that page as a template. Use DIST mode to check (Tab C), but write the formula and the substitution on your paper. The class boards show the professor expects working, not just answers.
  4. Sanity-check: a probability must be between 0 and 1, a variance must be ≥ 0, a pdf must integrate to 1, and probabilities in a table must sum to 1.
Every number in the worked examples and the answer key was recomputed by computer. Where a board photo had a slip, the corrected value is used and the slip is listed on the Traps and errata page (Tab X).
