General traps

  • Discrete: P(X < 3) = P(X ≤ 2). Continuous: ≤ and < are the same.
  • Without replacement from a small lot → Hypergeometric, not Binomial.
  • Independent ≠ mutually exclusive.
  • P(A | B) ≠ P(B | A). Use Bayes to flip.
  • Var(aX + b) = a²Var(X).
  • Exponential: λ = 1/mean.
  • Round z to 2 decimals for the table only.
  • Indonesian numbers: 0,4 = 0.4 and 15.015 = 15015.
